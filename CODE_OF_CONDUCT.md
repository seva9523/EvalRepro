# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free collaboration environment.

Be constructive, assume good intent while evaluating evidence critically, respect privacy and
licensing constraints, and focus review on the work rather than the contributor. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, deliberate humiliation, doxxing, sexualised conduct, and repeated disruption
are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through the contact method in
`SECURITY.md`. The maintainer may edit or remove content, close or lock threads, reject contributions,
or temporarily/permanently restrict participation. Reports will be handled as confidentially as
practical, with conflicts of interest disclosed and delegated where possible.
