## Adoption checkpoints

The project will be narrowed or integrated upstream rather than maintained as a vanity project if it
does not obtain meaningful external use. Initial checkpoints are:

- 30–45 days: three external testers, one external contributor, and two real framework adapters;
- 90 days: five external users/repositories, three external contributors, and one upstream reference.

These are operating targets, not claims of current adoption.
